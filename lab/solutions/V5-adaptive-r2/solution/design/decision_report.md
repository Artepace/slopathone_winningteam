# Decision approach and evidence limits

## Evidence for revision r2

Two of three r1 sessions ended at month 24 with a capability dismissal. In three L1 quarterly allocations across those two sessions, high-intensity work at a familiar Output level left Output flat while Health fell. The same action raised Output elsewhere, including at L1 from a lower starting value. Revision r2 tests a conditional choice based on realised returns. A missed promotion or weak grade alone does not reveal the cause or a numerical threshold. The rest of this document records the original r1 design rationale.

## What the task requires

The published rules describe a 48-month career, quarterly Energy/main actions, six-month review opportunities, multiple visible statistics, and several failure directions. Exact performance, promotion, failure, and hidden-risk formulas are not public. A strong policy therefore needs a memory of unresolved evidence, feasible protection, sustained development, and adaptation when the outcome contradicts its forecast.

V5 adaptive r1 replaces the earlier instruction architecture rather than modifying a single threshold. Its five Skills use only the handbook and current-run observations/options/logs. Prior run analysis shaped general procedures; event answers, hidden numerical values, or specific event sequences are not transferred into gameplay instructions.

## Documented failures and changes

The local reports used for diagnosis are `analysis_results3_final/REVIEW.md`, `analysis_results_20261029_local/REVIEW_RU.md`, and `analysis_results_v4_20261029/REVIEW_RU.md`. They distinguish source versions and tool success from game success. Their fingerprints are recorded outside the play package in the candidate provenance file.

The three preserved V3 runs scored 65.17, 26.24, and 50.35 (mean 47.25; two complete careers). One preserved V4 run scored 27.60 and ended after 36 months; the legacy long-reasoning version scored 19.48 and ended after 24 months. These are different observed runs with unequal sample sizes. They motivate diagnosis, not a controlled estimate of what V5 would score. In particular, V4 did take protective actions, so absence of protection is not a sufficient explanation of that result.

## Strategy in operation

Maintain current facts, unresolved concerns, and provisional interpretations as distinct information. For each story, reject unsupported safety claims and select a feasible option with durable value. For each quarter, reserve necessary protection before optional development, then combine the main action with a useful affordable Energy bundle. At checkpoints, use review explanations and observed directions to revise a provisional focus. A missed promotion alone cannot identify a hidden formula; no net growth alone cannot establish saturation.

Urgency is not an instruction to spend indefinitely without observing. Record each response, inspect its result, and adjust intensity only with relevant feedback. A watch state can use dedicated Energy treatment while keeping delivery/capability work in the main action. Serious unresolved warnings receive stronger protection, unless another threat is more immediately fatal. This balances two observed hazards: forgetting the warning and allowing a defensive policy to undermine performance. The actual balance may still be too conservative.

## What is new, and what is not claimed

All five Skill texts and their roles were written for this candidate. The new architecture combines several interventions, so it is a broad strategy experiment, not an isolated test of one V4 fix. Existing V3, V4, V3-risk-r1, and V4-risk-memory-r1 artifacts remain separate. Previously measured scores do not belong to V5.

The book sources are lawful public excerpts, publisher descriptions, and author/publisher companion material; they are not represented as five complete books read cover to cover. They supply decision concepts, not privileged knowledge of this simulator. Our implementation and its tradeoffs are our own inference.

A manual desk review uses invented situations to inspect the priorities and recovery procedure. It does not measure the designated model's adherence, game survival, score, or token efficiency.
