# Threat control

Distinguish a warning, a protective action, and evidence of recovery. Hidden risk has no observable numerical value unless the current interface explicitly publishes one. Do not invent thresholds or a guaranteed cure.

## Persistent risk state

Keep `risk`, its short evidence/month, and `response` in the ledger. These are our policy states, not engine states.

| State | Evidence and update |
| --- | --- |
| `unknown` | Relevant history is unavailable. Recover it when possible; uncertainty alone does not prove a crisis. |
| `clear` | No recorded unresolved warning, or specific feedback explicitly resolves the previously warned risk. This means no known warning, not zero hidden risk. |
| `watch` | Specific mild warning, or explicit feedback that an earlier urgent risk has reduced but remains present. Keep the concern unresolved. |
| `urgent` | Specific serious accumulated-risk warning or an actual risk incident. Keep urgent unless risk-specific feedback supports a downgrade or closure. |

Silence, generic praise, good statistics, promotion, and an accepted protection do not downgrade any state. A positive message about another concern does not resolve this one. Record protection as `response=month/action; effect=pending|partial|resolved`, separately from risk state. A partial improvement permits `urgent -> watch` only when feedback specifically supports it.

Track other failure directions independently: falling/low Health or Dignity, persistently zero Network, persistently negative Wealth, repeated weak performance, and stated skill inadequacy. A high value in one dimension cannot cancel another warning. Use trends and specific feedback; do not invent how many low observations cause failure.

## Protection requirements

1. First handle the most immediate evidenced threat that cannot wait until the next decision. For example, an unaffordable or immediately health-exhausting action is not made safe by its risk-reduction purpose. Prefer a feasible action helping several threatened dimensions. Explain an unavoidable conflict briefly.
2. At an eligible quarter with `urgent` risk, reserve the currently listed dedicated risk-treatment Energy action and the protective main action when both are feasible. The published menu calls them Defuse legacy risk and Lay low and reduce risk. Read their current costs; do not assign an unpublished amount of risk reduction. Another immediate survival threat can override the order or prevent part of this plan, but not erase the obligation or the warning.
3. With `watch`, include an affordable relevant protective measure this quarter. Prefer a measure whose CURRENT feedback or description supports the warned risk. Ordinarily use the dedicated Energy treatment while keeping a growth/performance main action; a protective main action can cover this when Energy is needed for a more immediate threat. Generic honest conduct or recovery alone does not prove hidden-risk treatment. If no suitable action is feasible, record the deferral and avoid adding exposure.
4. With `clear`, do not automatically buy protection every quarter. With `unknown`, recover evidence and prefer reversible, low-exposure choices; do not impose the urgent plan without evidence.

Execute a selected protective action once before reassessing its result. Do not blindly repeat it within the same quarter to chase an invisible number. At the next quarter, unresolved urgency still requires attention. Repeated treatment without specific improvement calls for a different relevant measure and closer attention to current feedback, not a claim of resolution.

## Balance protection and development

Use remaining resources for the most consequential untreated concern, then growth. `watch` must not silently become `clear`, but it need not consume both the Energy budget and main action. Weak reviews remain an active survival concern even while hidden risk is treated. At each quarter record what protection displaced and reconsider that opportunity cost using fresh evidence. No permanent emergency merely because a statistic is small but stable; no abandonment of a specific unresolved warning merely to pursue promotion.
