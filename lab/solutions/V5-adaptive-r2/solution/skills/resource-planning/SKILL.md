# Resource planning

Build one feasible quarterly plan. The plan combines Energy and the main action; do not optimize them in isolation. Follow the current menu and phase, including any changed costs or effects. Never infer an action ID from a previous menu.

## Build the plan

1. Read current Energy, choices, action costs/effects, unresolved concerns, latest review feedback, and the quarter baseline. Use `feedback-learning` to select a provisional development focus if it is stale or absent.
2. Reserve the protections required by `threat-control`. Check simultaneous threats before spending: preserve the ability to take an unaffordable-later protective action. Recovery may precede risk treatment when it prevents more immediate harm. Do not let optional growth spend a reserved budget.
3. Choose a main-action candidate that addresses the priority and complements the Energy plan. An urgent risk plan ordinarily uses the protective main action. Otherwise use current evidence to choose delivery/reporting for weak results or visibility, learning/mentorship/rotation for capability, relationship repair for damaged standing, or a broad project when no narrower constraint is supported. Read the displayed effects; action names alone are not a proof of hidden benefits.
4. Allocate the remaining Energy to unmet needs. Consider a small set of feasible bundles: needed recovery plus development; targeted learning plus an affordable complement; or delivery and relationships. Prefer a bundle supporting the leading evidenced constraint while avoiding deterioration elsewhere. If no constraint is supported, develop Skill, Network, and Output over successive quarters rather than maximize the smallest raw number; those quantities have different units and unknown requirements.
5. Check the whole bundle's costs and adverse effects before the first action. Include the opportunity cost of protection and the Health/Dignity cost of intensive work or learning. Favor an alternative with comparable supported benefits and fewer costs; do not assume undisclosed magnitudes. Maintain recovery capacity instead of assuming future events will repair damage.

## Before paying for delivery

When a weak review or a delivery warning is active, compare what the remaining bundles can actually add at this level and starting Output. Use the last observed return where available. If a safe one-Energy reporting action can cover the observed shortfall, take it first and observe. Preserve any Energy needed for urgent treatment or recovery. When a similar purchase left Output flat, prefer restoring depleted Health, addressing an outstanding risk, or repairing a documented collaboration gap. A main action can support delivery as well if its current effects and the other obligations allow it.

High-intensity work is an option when there is credible room for additional Output, its extra return over the cheaper feasible plan matters, and its displayed Health, Dignity and risk costs are manageable. If a comparable recent push left Output flat and consumed Health, skip that push until the starting conditions change. After a promotion or a fall in Output, reopen the comparison. Never discharge a protection obligation by pointing to an unrelated visible gain.

## Spending and feedback

- Execute one listed action, then observe. Keep the remainder of the plan if still feasible and consistent with the result. Replan only for new warnings, unexpected effects, changed menus/resources, a review, or a resolved concern. A changed choice number alone needs remapping, not a new strategy essay.
- Spend useful expiring Energy while respecting reservations. Passing is allowed when remaining actions are unaffordable, futile, or cause greater supported harm. An action benefiting only a statistic already at its PUBLISHED maximum is futile; an unchanged statistic is not proof of a maximum.
- Repeat an action only when the new menu still allows it and it still serves a documented need. A flat immediate result calls for checking delays and offsetting losses; do not repeat an identical zero-observed-benefit plan indefinitely, and do not prohibit all future investment in that dimension.
- On entering the main-action phase, re-check the current state and choose one available main action. A planned main action is not already executed. Record remaining obligations if the interface changes phases before they can be met.

## Review timing and endgame

The public horizon is 48 months with six-month reviews. Use the actual observed sequence: do not assume a review occurs before or after a particular action merely from the month number. Build sustained progress while future reviews remain; do not rely on a last-turn sprint.

Late in the game, prefer benefits that can occur before the observed ending, with protection and Health/Dignity still relevant. Skill and Network are also published ending dimensions. Do not automatically stop learning, declare Output worthless, or maximize Health at any cost in month 48. Consider a learning action's Health cost against its supported within-horizon benefit. If the final review is explicitly already settled, do not buy an action solely for a promotion window that no longer exists. Continue to the actual terminal state.
