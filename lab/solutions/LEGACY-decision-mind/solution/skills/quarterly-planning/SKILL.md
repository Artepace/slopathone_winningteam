# Quarterly Planning

The quarter-end phase is your strongest long-term lever: 16 quarters, each with 3 Energy points and one main action.
Plan the whole quarter first, then execute it one action at a time.

## 1. How the phase works
- You get 3 Energy points. Unused points are lost at the end of the quarter.
- Energy actions are usually taken **one per turn**. After each one, Energy goes down and you are asked again.
- An option to reserve or stop ends the Energy phase. Use it only when nothing useful is left (section 5).
- The main action is one choice per quarter, or none.
- **Read the description of every action in the current interface.** If a description says an action raises risk (for example hidden risk), treat that as a real cost. If it says an action lowers risk, it is your risk defence. The interface description wins over this page.

Published effects from the handbook:

| Energy action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| High-intensity work | 2 | Output up, Health down, Dignity down |
| Skill learning | 2 | Skill up, Health down |
| Network maintenance | 1 | Network up, Dignity up |
| Defuse legacy risk | 2 | no direct stat effect; aimed at old risk |
| Mental recovery | 1 | Dignity up |
| Health recovery | 1 | Health up |
| Prepare report materials | 1 | Output up, Dignity up |

| Main action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apply for a key project | Output, Skill, Network up |
| Report results to your manager | Output, Network, Dignity up |
| Fight for resources | Output, Network up |
| Apply for rotation | Skill, Network up |
| Showcase results | Output, Dignity up |
| Build a mentorship | Skill, Network up |
| Repair a key relationship | Network, Dignity up |
| Lay low and reduce risk | Health up |

## 2. Plan the quarter (before the first action)
Write the plan in the `next:` field of the ledger note (`decision-mind` section 10) on the first action of the phase, for example:
`M21 L3 C4/prove | H8= D7= S40+ N15= O18+ W9+ | P:none | Prepare report materials, visible Output before review | pred: O+ D+ | next: Network maintenance + Mental recovery, main: Showcase results`

**Step 1. Which quarter is it?**
- **Prove quarter:** the next six-month review comes within the next three months (use `next review: M<month>` from your notes; before the first review, expect it around month 6). Goal: visible Output, and Health and Dignity with a safe margin going into the review.
- **Build quarter:** otherwise. Goal: Skill and Network growth, recovery if needed.
- **Final year (months 37 to 48):** protect the ending stats and avoid new risk, whatever the quarter type.

**Step 2. Pressure check.** Use `decision-mind` section 2. Note which stats are under pressure and which have a safe margin.

**Step 3. Cap rule and buffer rule.**
- Health and Dignity cannot go above 10. Never spend Energy on an action whose only benefit is a stat that is already at its maximum. Spend that Energy on growth instead.
- When Health and Dignity both have a safe margin, they are a buffer to spend, not to protect. A career that ends with full Health and Dignity but a low level wasted its buffer. Use the buffer in build quarters and restore it before prove quarters and reviews.
- Health and Dignity refill quickly (a recovery action gives a solid boost for 1 Energy). If they have sat at their maximum for several quarters, you are over-protecting: stop buying recovery and put all 3 Energy points into growth.
- Check the stats line before you pick each action. A recovery action for a stat that is already at its maximum is a wasted point.

**Step 3b. Growth balance and missed promotions.** Promotion considers Skill, Network and Output together, and needs sustained results.
- Skill is an input to promotion and an ending dimension, and Skill learning is the only Energy action built for it. Make **Skill learning** part of every build quarter by default, unless Health is under pressure.
- Look at your notes: which of Skill, Network and Output has grown least over the last two quarters? Give it a share of this plan too. Compare each stat with its own recent growth, not with the other stats' raw numbers.
- If a review gives a good grade (A or B) but no promotion, a good grade is not enough. Do not change the whole plan. Keep growing all three, keep Skill learning every build quarter, and make Output visible before the next review.
- Do not repeat the identical plan for more than two quarters in a row. Vary the main action and the 1-point actions when the stats have changed.

**Step 4. Risk hygiene.** If, since your last Defuse legacy risk, you took High-intensity work, any action described as raising risk, or any story option you rated `risk: high`, then include Defuse legacy risk in this quarter's plan (or the next build quarter at the latest). Also include it when a warning or a comment in the monthly feed says risk is rising. If no such thing has happened, do not spend Energy on it.

**Step 5. Choose a template** (the first one that matches, adapt to the actions actually listed):

| Situation | Energy plan (3 points) | Main action |
|---|---|---|
| Health under pressure | Health recovery + Mental recovery or Network maintenance + Prepare report materials | Lay low and reduce risk |
| Dignity under pressure | Mental recovery + Network maintenance + Prepare report materials | Repair a key relationship, or Showcase results |
| Risk hygiene needed (step 4) | Defuse legacy risk + one 1-point action for the weakest stat | the best fit for the quarter type |
| Network at or near zero | Network maintenance + two 1-point actions for the weakest stats | Repair a key relationship, or Build a mentorship |
| Final year, safe margins | Prepare report materials + Network maintenance + one 1-point action for the weakest stat (never a recovery for a stat at its maximum) | Report results to your manager, or Showcase results |
| Build quarter, safe margins (the usual case) | Skill learning (2) + Network maintenance (1) | Apply for a key project; Build a mentorship or Apply for rotation when Skill is the weakest |
| Prove quarter, safe margins | Skill learning (2) + Prepare report materials (1), or Prepare report materials + Network maintenance + one 1-point action for the weakest stat | Report results to your manager, or Showcase results |

Rules for every template:
- High-intensity work raises hidden risk (the interface says so). Do not use it by default. Use it only when Output has been the clear blocker for two promotion windows in a row, Health and Dignity both have a safe margin, and it is not the final year. Schedule Defuse legacy risk in the next quarter, and never use it two quarters in a row.
- Do not repeat the same Health-costing action every quarter. Pair Skill learning with a recovery action only when Health has no safe margin.
- If a template action is not listed, replace it with the listed action that best serves the same stat.
- If the interface lets you take the same action twice, that is allowed when it still serves a stat that is below its maximum.
- In the first year, try each safe, useful action at least once (Skill learning, Network maintenance, Prepare report materials and the growth main actions) and record a `lesson:` about its real effect. High-intensity work and Defuse legacy risk follow their own rules above and are not part of this. Later quarters should use what the lessons show works best.

**Step 6. Check the plan with decision-mind.** Run the ruin filter (step C) on the plan as a whole: it must not lower any stat that is under pressure, and it must not leave Health or Dignity without a safe margin before a review.

## 3. Choose the main action
Match it to the need, in this order:
1. Health under pressure: Lay low and reduce risk.
2. Dignity or Network under pressure: Repair a key relationship.
3. Prove quarter: Showcase results, or Report results to your manager (also adds Network).
4. Build quarter: Apply for a key project for broad growth; Build a mentorship or Apply for rotation when Skill is the weakest.
5. Nothing fits: take no main action.

## 4. Execute
1. Take the planned actions one at a time with `take_action`, using the numbers exactly as listed.
2. After each action, work out the new Energy (from your own count or from `observe`, see section 5b). If something changed unexpectedly, call `observe` and re-plan the remaining points.
3. Every note uses the ledger format from `decision-mind` section 10. Put the remaining plan in the `next:` field, for example `next: Mental recovery, then main: Showcase results`.

## 5. When to reserve Energy
Choose the reserve or stop option only when every remaining affordable action would either lower a stat under pressure, or only raise stats that are already at their maximum. Otherwise spend all 3 points, because unused Energy is lost.

## 5b. Saving calls
After an Energy action, the result usually says only that it worked. Energy starts at 3 each quarter and each action subtracts its cost, so you may track it yourself. When the list of Energy actions is unchanged, you may take your next planned Energy action right away without a new `observe`. Call `observe` before the main action, and whenever anything looks unexpected.

## 6. After the quarter
In your next note, record what the quarter changed, and add a `lesson:` if an action did more or less than you expected (`decision-mind` section 8).
